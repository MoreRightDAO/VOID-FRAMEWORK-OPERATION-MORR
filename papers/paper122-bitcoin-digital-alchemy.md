## XI. Limitations

**Scope restriction.** This paper analyzes Bitcoin's protocol layer, not its market behavior. Market-layer drift dynamics (Pe elevation through speculative attention) are documented in Paper 7 and explicitly excluded here. The distinction between protocol and market is crisp for Bitcoin but may be harder to maintain for chains with on-chain governance, where the protocol itself can be modified through stakeholder processes — making the "prohibition" softer and O potentially nonzero.

**Subjective framework components.** The O, R, α assignments for fiat money and Bitcoin are derived scores rather than computed measurements. The claim that O_bitcoin ≈ 0 requires accepting that "anyone can run a full node" constitutes full transparency — a reasonable but contested position. Users who lack the technical capacity to verify the code face a practically nonzero O even if the theoretical O is zero. Framework application to protocol layers is less standardized than application to user-facing platforms.

**ATHANOR predictions are pending.** Seven predictions are registered. Four (BTC-P1 through BTC-P4) are testable from public Bitcoin data but have not been formally computed for this paper. Three (ATH-P1 through ATH-P3) are live on-chain but at n < 30. The paper's empirical grounding is the sovereignty void cross-cultural analysis (n=128, Fisher p < 10⁻¹⁸) and the structural isomorphism inventory — not a completed prediction test. The paper registers predictions and establishes the structural framework; empirical closure awaits data accumulation.

**Satoshi opacity.** Section VIII explicitly acknowledges that the framework cannot determine whether Satoshi encoded the thermodynamic structure intentionally or intuitively. This is not a limitation of the isomorphism claims — those hold regardless of intent — but it limits the strength of any claim about Bitcoin's design being "thermodynamically informed." The structural correspondences are real; the design intent is permanently unknown.

**Substrate sensitivity.** ATHANOR runs on MegaETH testnet. The protection architecture's durability claims assume immutable smart contract deployment on a live mainnet. Testnet contracts can be redeployed. The claim that "the Malleus Maleficarum cannot close a smart contract" applies to mainnet deployment; testnet deployment is experimental infrastructure. Mainnet migration is pending.
