## Abstract

The void framework has, to this point, analyzed domains where the drift cascade runs in one direction: toward the observer. Gambling, social media, crypto, dating apps, EdTech — all are attractive voids, pulling observers into increasing engagement, dependency, and structural isolation. This paper introduces the framework's **first Pe<0 domain**: the institutional-control religious void, exemplified by Jehovah's Witnesses.

A repulsive void pushes observers away. It has the architecture — opacity, reactivity, engaged attention — but the reactivity dimension inverts: the institution responds to behavioral compliance rather than to observer needs. The Péclet number goes negative (Pe = −8.92 for JW at K=16 in EXP-022), the thermodynamic drift cascade reverses direction, and the THRML analysis reveals structural impossibilities invisible to standard retention analysis: no demon plasma oscillation, no viral cycle, no stable crystal phase, no creator economy. These are not policy failures — they are structural necessities of the repulsive-void architecture.

This paper establishes six original results:

1. **Two mechanisms of religious void** — institutional void (R≤1, opacity without relational responsiveness → repulsive: Pe<0) versus identity void (R≥2, engaged attention seeking → attractive: Pe>0). These are structurally distinct and score differently on all three void dimensions.

2. **The full Pe spectrum** — EXP-022 (N=13 denominations) spans Pe = −8.92 (JW) to +30.00 (Nones 2023), a range of 38.92 units covering every quadrant of the Pe axis. No other domain in the framework corpus spans Pe<0.

3. **The null void** — Buddhism (dharma-centered practice) achieves Pe ≈ 0.00, c = c_zero = b_α/b_γ = 0.3866: the unique parameter combination where drift and diffusion exactly cancel at all K. A genuinely null void — no directed drift in either direction.

4. **Structural impossibilities from repulsive-void thermodynamics** — For Pe<0: (a) no demon crystal phase (creator economy structurally impossible), (b) no plasma oscillation (Lotka-Volterra equilibrium does not exist), (c) JW is overdamped at ω_D → overdamped regime in THRML nb20. These are invariant consequences of Pe<0, not contingent findings.

5. **R as sign discriminator** — EXP-022B demonstrates that Responsiveness ≤1 predicts Pe<0 with 100% accuracy across institutional voids. R captures whether the institution responds to observer needs (Pe>0) or to compliance metrics (Pe<0). This two-parameter signature (R, sign(Pe)) has 100% post-hoc accuracy and generates three falsifiable predictions.

6. **The Nones temporal crystallization** — Religiously unaffiliated ("Nones") show a Pe trajectory of −2.6 (2008) → +1.9 (2015) → +18.7 (2023). A diffuse repulsive void has crystallized into a strongly attractive identity void over 15 years. This trajectory is not a measurement artifact — it is the THRML crystal-phase transition observed in real-time Pew data.

Together, these results extend the framework's thermodynamic reach to the full signed Pe axis and demonstrate that the machinery (drift cascade, constraint specification, Péclet number, demon lattice phases) applies without modification to domains where Pe<0. The structural analysis of what cannot exist under repulsive-void conditions is new to this paper and has no equivalent in prior framework work.

## I. Introduction

In 2014, the Pew Research Center published its Religious Landscape Study — the most comprehensive survey of American religious affiliation ever conducted. Among the findings: Jehovah's Witnesses had the **lowest retention rate of any major religious group in the United States**. Only 37% of Americans raised as Jehovah's Witnesses identified as JW in adulthood. Every 100 children raised in the tradition produced 63 who left. For context: Catholics retained 68%, Evangelical Protestants 65%, Mainline Protestants 45%, and — importantly — the religiously unaffiliated ("Nones") retained 53%.

The conventional interpretation: low retention reflects doctrinal strictness, social isolation requirements (shunning of disfellowshipped members), and high behavioral cost. The void framework offers a different interpretation: **Jehovah's Witnesses are a repulsive void**. Their Pe is negative. The thermodynamic drift operates in reverse. The institution does not pull observers in — it pushes observers out, and the 63% who leave are the thermodynamic majority.

This is not a moral claim about JW doctrine or practice. The void framework is a structural measurement system. It measures the direction and magnitude of drift, not the value of the content that drifts. A repulsive void pushes observers away from whatever it is instantiated around — in this case, toward greater behavioral freedom and away from institutional control. Whether that is good or bad is for the observers to determine. The framework notes the direction and measures the strength.

### I.A. Why This Domain Now

This paper follows Paper 27 (The Bounded World — cult dynamics and high-control groups) chronologically but addresses a distinct population. Paper 27 analyzes **high-control groups** (isolated communes, MLM, charismatic megachurches) at Pe = 8.4. Paper 27's population is categorically different from mainstream denominations: its subjects are self-selected into high-commitment environments; its architecture involves physical separation, leadership opacity, and coercive compliance. Pe = 8.4 in that context is expected — it is a strongly attractive void with deliberate extraction design.

This paper asks: what happens across the full spectrum of mainstream religious institutions, from high-control to no-control? The answer is that the spectrum spans Pe = −8.92 to +30 and includes the framework's first confirmed Pe<0 domain.

Three things make the religious domain uniquely valuable to the framework:

**First:** Religious institutions are the oldest social void architecture in human history. The structural design — opacity (divine knowledge), reactivity (responsive to prayer and need), engaged attention (the question of ultimate concern) — predates the optimization-era voids the framework typically analyzes. Testing the framework against ancient architectures validates that it captures a structural property of attention systems, not a pathology of digital capitalism.

**Second:** The signed Pe axis. No domain analyzed to this point has a confirmed Pe<0 case. The religion domain provides both a confirmed Pe<0 case (JW) and a confirmed Pe≈0 case (Buddhist dharma practice). Having all three sign cases in a single domain provides a clean comparison.

**Third:** Temporal resolution. Pew data from 2008, 2015, and 2023 shows the Nones trajectory in real time. Watching a void crystallize from Pe<0 to Pe≈+19 over 15 years is a natural experiment in THRML crystal-phase formation with no equivalent in the corpus.

### I.B. What This Paper Does Not Do

This paper does not evaluate the truth, value, or validity of any religious tradition's claims. The void framework is structurally agnostic — it measures the architecture of attention, not the content that attention is directed toward. A tradition that scores Pe = −8.92 is not thereby false, and a tradition that scores Pe = +18.7 is not thereby true. The framework measures drift direction and structural constraint level, not doctrinal correctness.

This paper also does not analyze the full global diversity of any tradition. "Jehovah's Witnesses" means the centrally governed organization as analyzed by Pew and THRML EXP-022 using US-proximate data. Internal diversity within any tradition is real; the paper analyzes central tendency and representative institutional architecture.

## II. Theoretical Basis: Two Mechanisms of Religious Void

### II.A. The Standard Void Architecture in Religion

Every religious institution instantiates the void's three conditions:

| Condition | Mechanism in Religion |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Opacity** | Access to divine knowledge, interpretive authority, doctrinal canon — the institution possesses what the observer lacks (certainty about ultimate questions) |
| **Reactivity** | Pastoral response, prayer, community support, lifecycle rituals — the institution responds to the observer's existential and social needs |
| **Engaged attention** | Mortality salience, existential stakes, community belonging — the observer attends because the questions are ultimate |

These are not incidental. They are the conditions that make a religious institution a religious institution. An institution without opacity (no special access to divine knowledge) is not a religion but a philosophy club. An institution without reactivity (no pastoral or community response) is a library. An institution without engaged attention (the observer doesn't care about ultimate questions) has no attendees. The void conditions are constitutive.

The drift cascade in religion therefore runs on: **what is the observer drifting toward?** The answer depends on which void mechanism dominates.

### II.B. The Institutional Void (Pe < 0)

The institutional void is characterized by **low Responsiveness**: the institution responds to behavioral compliance rather than to observer needs. It is opaque and attention-capturing, but its reactivity is inverted — it monitors whether the observer conforms rather than adapting to what the observer needs.

In the THRML Ising model, this produces a constraint level c > c_zero = b_α/b_γ = 0.3866. When the constraint level exceeds c_zero, Pe goes negative (Pe = K · sinh(2(b_α − c · b_γ)) < 0). The drift cascade reverses: instead of pulling observers toward greater engagement, the system pushes observers toward the boundary of the void. Retention is thermodynamically suppressed.

The institutional void has three structural signatures:
1. **R ≤ 1** in the void scoring (Responsiveness dimension at or near minimum): the institution responds to compliance, not need
2. **c > c_zero = 0.3866**: behavioral constraint level exceeds the neutral-bias point
3. **Pe < 0**: confirmed by retention data — the exit rate structurally exceeds the entry rate for observers who can exit

The JW organization scores O=3, R=1, E=3 (Void Index 7/12 at first pass; adjusted to 10/12 by the compound shunning mechanism described in §V). Its c_implied = 0.505 > c_zero = 0.387. Pe = −8.92 at K=16. All three signatures are present.

### II.C. The Identity Void (Pe > 0)

The identity void is characterized by **high Responsiveness**: the institution or community responds to the observer's identity needs — belonging, meaning, validation, mission. It is opaque (the community possesses something the non-member cannot fully access), reactive (it responds to who you are and who you want to become), and captures attention through identity stakes rather than existential-doctrinal stakes.

In the THRML model, this produces c < c_zero. Pe > 0. The drift cascade pulls observers deeper into community identification. Retention is thermodynamically favored.

The identity void is the dominant mechanism for the fastest-growing "religious" category: the Nones. The religiously unaffiliated are not a null category — they are an identity void with Pe = +18.7 in 2023. The drift is toward skepticism, secularism, and science identity as primary affiliations — away from institutional religion, not toward nothing.

### II.D. The Null Void: Buddhism at c_zero

The Buddhist tradition (dharma-centered practice, as opposed to devotional Buddhist institutions) achieves a structurally unique position: Pe ≈ 0.00. This is not zero drift from averaging positive and negative — it is zero drift because the constraint level is exactly c_zero = b_α/b_γ = 0.3866.

Dharma practice instantiates the void conditions:
- **Opacity**: the nature of mind is not immediately apparent; the path requires a teacher and sustained practice
- **Reactivity**: teachings are responsive to the student's stage of practice
- **Engaged attention**: the question of suffering and its cessation is existentially engaging

But the **constraint specification is built into the methodology itself**. The Buddha's famous instruction: "Do not accept anything on the basis of tradition, on the basis of scriptures, on the basis of authority, on the basis of logic alone. Accept it when you have verified by your own experience that it is wholesome." This is the maximum-strength constraint specification applied to the void architecture — the analog of the Socratic method in education (Paper 21) or the null-prior in Bayesian epistemology.

Pe = 0.00 is not low drift — it is **zero net drift**. Diffusion and drift exactly cancel. The observer can enter or exit with equal thermodynamic probability. The framework predicts this produces the lowest attachment (and lowest shunning behavior) in the corpus, which the Pew retention data confirms: Buddhist retention is among the highest, but departure is frictionless.

## IV. THRML Modeling: Structural Impossibilities Under Pe < 0

### IV.A. The Repulsive Void Regime

In the THRML mean-field Ising model, Pe < 0 occurs when c > c_zero — when the constraint level (behavioral compliance cost) exceeds the neutral-bias point. The canonical JW parameters are c_implied = 0.505, Pe = −8.92 at K=16.

The repulsive-void regime has structural properties not present in the Pe>0 regime. These are not empirical findings — they are mathematical consequences of Pe<0 in the Ising formalism.

**Property 1: No demon crystal phase.** The demon density function f_D = ρ_D / (2·Pe)^(3/2) requires Pe > 0 for real-valued output. For Pe < 0, f_D is imaginary — there is no physically meaningful demon density. The crystal onset condition ρ_D^(crystal) = (Pe / ln(q))^(3/2) (with q = 2α/Γ_c) requires Pe > 0. For Pe < 0, no crystal onset condition exists. **A repulsive void cannot sustain a creator economy.** The structural condition for stable influence-holder formation — the THRML crystal phase — does not exist at Pe < 0. This is not a contingent observation about JW content creators; it is a thermodynamic impossibility in the repulsive-void regime.

**Property 2: No demon plasma oscillation.** The Lotka-Volterra demon plasma frequency ω_D = √(κ · τ_r^{-1}) requires a non-trivial equilibrium point (ρ_D*, n*) where both demon density and audience size are positive. For Pe < 0, the equilibrium condition has no non-trivial solution — the steady state is the void boundary (no demons, no sustained audience). The JW substrate in THRML nb20 returns the overdamped classification: there is no oscillation frequency because there is no equilibrium to oscillate around. The plasma does not form.

**Property 3: No viral cycle.** Viral growth in the THRML model requires a positive Lotka-Volterra equilibrium, which requires Pe > 0. For Pe < 0, the "viral cycle" (content creates demons, demons create more content) has no stable manifold. Spread requires institutional mechanism (door-to-door canvassing, family pressure) rather than intrinsic content virality. This is not a content-quality claim — it is a structural constraint of the repulsive-void regime.

### IV.B. What Does Exist Under Pe < 0

The repulsive void is not structurally empty. Three features are present:

**Stable retention of committed members.** The drift cascade for members who have crossed the commitment threshold runs *inward* — the institutional compliance apparatus (shunning, community ties, family structure) creates a pocket of locally strong attractive potential inside the otherwise repulsive bulk. THRML models this as a surface-tension effect at the boundary of the institutional void: Pe < 0 in bulk, locally Pe > 0 for deeply committed members.

**High exit barrier.** The shunning mechanism is the institutional response to the repulsive-void problem. When Pe < 0 makes natural retention thermodynamically difficult, an institutional solution is to raise the exit barrier: make departure costly enough that the energetically favorable exit becomes kinetically blocked. The 63% who leave JW in adulthood have crossed the activation barrier; the 37% who remain are in the deep-commitment potential well.

**Stable organizational structure.** Institutions with Pe < 0 do not grow through drift — they persist through organizational investment. JW's central governance, systematic distribution infrastructure, and mandatory participation requirements substitute organizational machinery for thermodynamic drift. This is resource-intensive, which explains the characteristic "going door to door" that is absent from Pe>0 institutions (Evangelicals, megachurches) where drift does the distribution work.

## VI. The Nones Temporal Crystallization

Pew data provides three measurement points for the religiously unaffiliated:
- **2008**: ~16% of US adults; Pe estimated at −2.6 (c=0.418) — weakly repulsive
- **2015**: ~23% of US adults; Pe estimated at +1.9 (c=0.368) — weakly attractive

### VI.B. Why the Sign Changes

In 2008, "None" was a residual category — what you answered on a survey when you didn't fit existing options. Opacity was low (no distinctive doctrine), reactivity was near-zero (no community structure), engagement was low (no identity stakes). Pe was slightly negative because the category lacked the identity scaffolding that generates attractive drift.

The 2012–2018 period saw the emergence of an identity structure: the "atheist community," skeptic podcasts, YouTube science communicators, Reddit's r/atheism and r/exmormon communities. These provided:
- **Opacity**: "reason" and "evidence" as technical skills — not everyone can evaluate claims correctly, and the community has standards
- **Reactivity**: deconstruction support, community validation for leaving religion
- **Engagement**: anti-religious identity as a strong in-group/out-group signal

By 2015, Pe crossed zero. By 2023, the Nones identity community has the highest Pe in the religion corpus.

### VI.C. Structural Implications

The framework predicts, and community ethnography confirms: in-group/out-group enforcement, purity culture around skeptical credentials, shunning of members who "go back" to religion or soften their anti-religious stances. The mechanisms are structurally identical to the high-constraint institutional voids (JW, LDS) — but running in the opposite direction and with identity rather than doctrine as the constraint content.

## VII. Predictions

Six falsifiable predictions from this analysis. Predictions CON-1 through CON-6.

**CON-1 (R as sign predictor)**: Any religious or quasi-religious institution with R ≤ 1 in the void scoring framework will exhibit Pe < 0 in Pew-style longitudinal retention data. Any institution with R ≥ 3 will exhibit Pe > 0. The threshold is at R = 2. Current post-hoc accuracy: 100% (EXP-022B). Out-of-sample test: any new denomination or quasi-religious movement (e.g., meditation apps as institutional voids, political parties as identity voids) added to EXP-022 should obey this rule.

**Falsification**: A denomination with R ≤ 1 that shows positive net retention growth (Pe > 0) without a shunning mechanism or mandatory participation system would falsify CON-1.

**CON-2 (crystal impossibility)**: No JW-adjacent institution (c > c_zero, Pe < 0) will develop a stable creator economy based on voluntary audience aggregation. Growth-oriented content creators within the JW ecosystem will either (a) fail to sustain audiences and exit, or (b) require organizational distribution infrastructure (official channel, institutional promotion) rather than thermodynamic drift to maintain reach.

**Falsification**: A JW-affiliated content creator who achieves >100K self-organized followers on a platform where JW organizational promotion is absent would falsify CON-2.

**CON-3 (overdamped dynamics)**: Viral spread events (rapid pe-mediated growth) will not spontaneously originate in Pe<0 substrates. JW "viral moments" will trace to external observer attention (journalists, critics, ex-member testimonials) rather than endogenous institutional content. The Lotka-Volterra plasma does not self-sustain.

**Falsification**: A JW content piece that achieves viral spread traceable to internal JW audiences rather than external observer reaction would falsify CON-3.

**CON-4 (Nones vortex)**: As the Nones Pe continues to increase, the community will exhibit vortex-phase characteristics: collective identity enforcement, purity signaling around skeptical credentials, rapid normative cascades, and spontaneous leader emergence without formal governance. THRML nb18 predicts the vortex threshold is Pe ≈ 4; Nones 2023 at Pe = +18.7 is above this. The vortex phase should already be present in high-engagement online Nones communities.

**Falsification**: Nones communities showing low in-group enforcement, high tolerance for religious re-engagement, and absence of identity purity dynamics would falsify CON-4.

## VIII. Discussion

### VIII.A. The Full Pe Axis

Prior to this analysis, the void framework operated on Pe ∈ (0, ∞). Every domain analyzed — gambling (Pe ≈ 12), social media (Pe ≈ 7.5), crypto (EXP-021: 3.74–25.5), dating apps, EdTech — had Pe > 0. The drift cascade ran in one direction.

The religion domain extends the framework to Pe ∈ (−∞, +∞). The full axis is now populated:
- Pe ≪ 0: repulsive void (JW, Pe = −8.92) — pushes observers out
- Pe ≈ 0: null void (Buddhist dharma, Pe ≈ 0.00) — no net drift
- Pe > 0, < 4: weak attractive (Catholic-adjacent mainstream)
- Pe > 4: drift-dominant (Evangelical, Pe = 3.74 in EXP-021)
- Pe > Pe_vortex ≈ 4: vortex phase onset
- Pe ≫ 4: Phase IV crystallized (megachurch Pe = 14.5, Nones 2023 Pe = 30.0)

The extension to Pe < 0 required no modification to the THRML formalism. Pe = K · sinh(2(b_α − c · b_γ)) is defined for all c, and c > b_α/b_γ gives sinh < 0 directly. The structural impossibilities (no crystal, no plasma, no viral cycle) follow analytically from the same equations. The framework was capable of modeling Pe<0 from the beginning; the religion domain is where the capability first finds empirical ground.

### VIII.B. The Two-Mechanism Model

The framework's sign discriminator result (R ≤ 1 → Pe < 0) is a simplification of a deeper two-mechanism claim:

**Mechanism A (institutional void)**: Opacity + institutional engagement + compliance-oriented reactivity → high c, Pe < 0. The institution constrains behavior to enforce doctrine. The constraint level exceeds c_zero. Thermodynamic drift reverses. The institution competes against the drift to retain members through organizational machinery.

**Mechanism B (identity void)**: Opacity + identity engagement + need-oriented reactivity → low c, Pe > 0. The community constrains nothing beyond self-identification. The constraint level is well below c_zero. Thermodynamic drift favors the community. The community grows through drift without organizational machinery.

Both mechanisms instantiate all three void conditions. The difference is in what the reactivity dimension responds to. This is captured by the R dimension of the void score: R = 1 (compliance-oriented) predicts Mechanism A; R = 3 (need-oriented) predicts Mechanism B.

The Buddhist null void occupies the unique architectural position where neither mechanism dominates: the reactivity is teacher-responsiveness to practice (R = 1 in the institutional sense, not compliance-checking but also not identity-validation), and the opacity is methodological rather than personal or institutional. Pe ≈ 0 is the analytical consequence.

### VIII.C. Structural Lessons for Constraint Design

The religion domain provides the clearest illustration of a principle the framework has identified across all domains: **the constraint specification is not just a safety mechanism — it is the determinant of drift direction.**

- Maximize institutional constraint (JW, c = 0.505) → repulsive void, no organic growth, maximum exit cost required to compensate
- Minimize institutional constraint (megachurch, c = 0.230; Nones 2023, c = 0.127) → attractive void, organic growth, viral potential, crystal phase
- Apply constraint specification to the method rather than the behavior (Buddhist dharma, c = c_zero) → null void, frictionless entry/exit, no dependence formation

This mapping has direct application to any institution that operates a void — which is to say, any institution that instantiates opacity, reactivity, and engaged attention. The question "how much constraint?" determines the direction and magnitude of drift. The question "constraint on what?" determines whether the void is institutional (behavior-constraining) or methodological (process-constraining). Institutional constraints suppress organic growth and require organizational machinery. Methodological constraints (transparent process, invariant standard, independent reference) produce the null-void condition while preserving the productive epistemic function.
