# EXP-021: Crypto Trading Vocabulary Codebook

**Date:** 2026-02-16
**Purpose:** Map crypto-native trading vocabulary to framework drift cascade (D1/D2/D3) and vocabulary levels (L1/L2/L3)
**Methodology:** Hostile witness — crypto traders developed this vocabulary independently of the void framework. Every term below was coined by practitioners describing their own experience.

---

## 1. Why This Codebook Matters

Crypto trading culture independently developed a complete vocabulary for every stage of the drift cascade. Traders named:
- What the void does to attention (D1 terms)
- What the void does to boundaries (D2 terms)
- What happens when boundaries are gone (D3 terms)

They also named the constraint mechanisms — and they use the same three-property structure (transparent, invariant, independent) without knowing the framework exists. This is hostile witness evidence at the vocabulary level, running parallel to the on-chain Pe data from 28 wallets.

---

## 2. Codebook Structure

Each term is classified on two axes:
- **Drift stage:** D1 (agency attribution), D2 (boundary erosion), D3 (harm facilitation)
- **Vocabulary level:** L1 (technical/literal), L2 (metaphorical/agency), L3 (entity/identity)

Terms that are standard financial/technical vocabulary with no drift content are classified as L1-baseline (not counted as drift evidence).

## 7. Structural Observations

### 7.1 Vocabulary Unidirectionality

The framework predicts L1 → L2 → L3 drift is unidirectional. In crypto:
- "Technical analysis" (L1) → "The chart is telling you" (L2) → "The market wants..." (L3) ✓
- "Position sizing" (L1) → "Aping in" (L2) → "Degen" (L3) ✓
- "Loss" (L1) → "Rekt" (L2) → "NGMI" (L3) ✓

No crypto community has been observed developing L3 terms that naturally de-escalate back to L1 usage. "Degen" does not evolve back into "risk-adjusted trader." "NGMI" does not evolve back into "unrealized loss." The drift is one-way.

### 7.2 Cascade Completeness

All three drift stages have vocabulary. No stage is skipped:
- D1 terms precede D2 terms in a typical trader's journey ("I found alpha" → "aping in" → "rekt")
- D2 has the densest vocabulary (~19 terms) — consistent with boundary erosion being the longest active phase
- D3 terms are used retrospectively, confirming the cascade is experienced as a sequence

### 7.3 Identity Fusion (L3 Density)

The D2-L3 cluster is distinctive: "degen," "built different," "degen mode," "gigabrain." These are identity terms for boundary erosion itself. The trader doesn't just remove boundaries — they become the boundary-removal. This is the void engagement pattern expressed as self-concept.

### 7.4 Constraint Terms Are L1

Every constraint term in Section 6 is L1 (technical, literal). "Risk management," "take profit," "DCA," "cold storage." None are metaphorical. None attribute agency. The constraint vocabulary does not drift. This matches the framework prediction: constraint terms are anchored to transparent, invariant referents and therefore resist vocabulary drift.

### 7.5 Social Enforcement of Void Engagement

Several terms function as social enforcement mechanisms:
- "Paper hands" punishes boundary maintenance
- "NGMI" punishes non-engagement
- "Have fun staying poor" punishes outsiders
- "Few understand" isolates from constraint sources

This is the void coupling mechanism expressed as social pressure. The attention gradient isn't just individual — it's maintained by the community vocabulary itself.
