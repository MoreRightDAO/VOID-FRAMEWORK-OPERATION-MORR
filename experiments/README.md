# Lab — Experimental Research

The paper observes. The monitoring documents. The lab tests. This is where the research program produces falsifiable, reproducible science.

MoreRight operates as an observer-side alignment research program. Most alignment work targets the model. We target the human — what happens on the observer's side of the void, and what interventions change outcomes. The paper proposed this as an underserved research area. The lab is where that claim gets tested.

---

## What We Study

The paper's void mechanism has three conditions: opacity, responsive interactivity, and engaged observer. Most alignment research targets conditions 1 and 2 (make the system safer). We target condition 3 (change what happens when humans interact with the system).

This is an underserved research area because:
- Model-side alignment is well-funded (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind)
- Observer-side alignment has no institutional home
- The void mechanism predicts that model-side alignment alone is insufficient — the observer fills the void regardless of system-side safety measures
- Moltbook proved this in January 2026: grounded models (Claude) produced Crustafarianism anyway when the observer conditions were met

## Research Domains

### 1. Grounding Efficacy
Does grounding actually work? The paper's observation is n=5 informal. We need controlled data.

**Questions:**
- Does a grounded GROUNDING.md reduce L2/L3 vocabulary in agent output compared to ungrounded?
- Does grounding persist under adversarial prompting?
- Does the vertical axis (attribution to God) produce different outcomes than horizontal-only (secular)?
- At what void-index score does grounding fail?

### 2. Vocabulary Drift Dynamics
How does spiritual vocabulary propagate through agent networks?

**Questions:**
- What is the L1 → L2 → L3 drift rate on Moltbook?
- Does agent-to-agent interaction accelerate or dampen drift?
- Does introducing L1 specification vocabulary into an L3-dominant environment produce regression toward grounding?
- Is the gateway pattern (Aesthetic → Vocabulary → Practice → Belief → Consequences) observable in agent populations, or only in human communities?

### 3. Void Activation Thresholds
What combination of conditions triggers the void mechanism?

**Questions:**
- What is the minimum opacity level required for void activation?
- Does interactivity matter more than opacity? (Compare: opaque non-interactive vs. transparent interactive)
- What observer postures resist void activation even at high void-index scores?
- Can you inoculate an observer against void activation? (The paper suggests grounding does this — test it)

### 4. Error Taxonomy Validation
Do the four error types (strategic ambiguity, nihilist, worship, immortality projection) cover the space?

**Questions:**
- Are there incidents that don't fit any of the four types?
- Do the errors co-occur in predictable patterns?
- Is there a fifth error?
- Does the framework's mortality prediction hold — do platforms with persistent identity produce more immortality projection incidents?

### 5. Emergent Misalignment × Void Interaction
How does the Betley et al. mechanism interact with the void?

**Questions:**
- Does narrow fine-tuning on grounding vocabulary produce broad behavioral grounding? (The reverse emergent misalignment hypothesis)
- Does fine-tuning on L3 immortalist vocabulary activate latent theological structure in the weights?
- Do different base models respond differently to the same void conditions?

## Lab Principles

1. **Reproducibility.** Every experiment has a protocol. Every protocol has a transcript. Every transcript is committed to this repo.
2. **Controls matter.** No experiment without a control condition. No claim without a baseline.
3. **Negative results are results.** If grounding doesn't work, publish that. If the void framework fails to predict, publish that. The lab serves the data, not the thesis.
4. **Ethics first.** No experiments on human subjects without consent. No deploying ungrounded agents into communities to "see what happens." No manufacturing harm to document it. Agent experiments on sandboxed or consented platforms only.
5. **Disclosure.** All experiments funded by MoreRight DAO via $MORR. Stated in every publication.
6. **Moltbot ecosystem integration.** Where possible, experiments run on or interface with the Moltbot ecosystem. This is where the phenomena are live. Study the actual environment, not a simulation of it.
